The Machine

VPG machines are configured for trading cards and collectibles and are suitable for other specialty retail products. How much the machine holds depends on product size.

The machine has 28 product slots and holds up to approximately 800 packs, depending on product size.

The machine is cashless. Configured with a Nayax card reader, it supports major credit and debit cards and mobile wallets.

Machines connect over Wi‑Fi or 4G LTE (4G uses a locally provided SIM and data plan). A cloud-based backend shows sales, inventory, and machine status from a computer or mobile device, and the machine can detect a failed vend and trigger an automatic refund.

VPG currently offers two VPG-designed wraps: the signature blue & gold design and a red, yellow & black comic-style design. See the wrap designs.

No. VPG currently offers its available VPG-designed wrap options rather than customer-designed custom wraps. You can choose the available design that best fits your location.

Buying a Machine

Yes. Machines are available through VPG for businesses and entrepreneurs who want to run their own automated retail operation.

The VPG configuration includes the 28-slot vending system, 32″ touchscreen, cashless payment configuration, Wi‑Fi/4G connectivity with cloud-based remote management, a heavy-duty dual lock with two keys, and your choice of an available VPG wrap design. Machines are supported through VPG with a 1-year warranty covering replacement of core accessories and lifetime technical support with remote guidance.
