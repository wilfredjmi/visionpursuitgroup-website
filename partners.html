The Partnership

Designed to Be Easy for Your Team

Our location-partner model is intended to add an eye-catching retail amenity without requiring your staff to manage product selection, restocking, or routine machine operations.

Minimal Day-to-Day Involvement

VPG handles normal machine operation, stocking, inventory management, and monitoring, so your staff can stay focused on your business.

Unique Customer Amenity

A bold trading-card vending experience adds something memorable and visually engaging to the location.

Engaging Point of Interest

The interactive touchscreen and eye-catching VPG wrap give customers something to notice, browse, and explore.

Inventory Management

We manage loading, replenishment, and ongoing inventory tracking.

Regular Restocking

Selection That Evolves
